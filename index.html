Our Story

Compassion in action,
since 2021.

In 2021, Mr. Santosh Aher and Mrs. Sheetal Aher witnessed a reality they couldn't ignore in our local communities.

Anath Niradhar Sevabhavi Sanstha was born from the belief that family isn't just blood—it's who cares for you when you are down. We are a registered NGO working tirelessly to bridge the gap between privilege and poverty.

What We Do

Comprehensive care for
sustainable change.

Education

We sponsor school fees, uniforms, and books for children who are at risk of dropping out due to financial constraints.

Nutrition

Daily food drives in slum areas and support for local orphanages ensuring no child sleeps on an empty stomach.

Healthcare

Free medical camps, cataract surgeries for the elderly, and emergency medical fund assistance.
